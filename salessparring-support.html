Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, open the Sales Sparring app, go to Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account.

Your data will be permanently removed immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sales Sparring work?

Sales Sparring connects you with an AI buyer persona for live voice practice. After each call, you receive a scorecard with coaching feedback.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your call data is stored securely and is never shared with third parties. You can delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

What microphone permission is needed?

Sales Sparring requires microphone access for the AI voice practice calls. This is only used during active practice sessions.
